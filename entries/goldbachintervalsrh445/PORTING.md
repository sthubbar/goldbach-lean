# Provenance of the LeanLab files

`LeanLab/` is the part of Steve Hubbard's Lean Lab that this entry needs: 137 modules, the import
closure of `LeanLab.Routes.U1508Compose445`, taken from the lab's Lean v4.35.0 tree as published
in github.com/sthubbar/lean-lab-preprints, Paper A. AI agents, Claude by Anthropic, wrote them
between 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-25, except as follows.

## Ported code

* `LeanLab/PNTPort/StrongPNT/`, 16 files, from PrimeNumberTheoremAnd commit
  c39a751132c88b6e8080b74c74023fd95b3d8be0, Apache-2.0, written by the PrimeNumberTheoremAnd
  project contributors: the zero-free region for ζ, Mellin calculus, residue calculus on
  rectangles, bounds for ζ and the strong prime number theorem. Each file carries a notice. These
  files are byte for byte the copies in `entries/goldstonsuriajaya2021`, whose PORTING.md lists
  their changes: `@[blueprint ...]` attributes and `import Architect` removed, import paths
  renamed, `Tactic/LinearCombinationPrime.lean` re-exporting Mathlib's
  `Mathlib.Tactic.LinearCombinationPrime`, MellinCalculus `Smeas` restored, an explicit
  measurability term in EulerMaclaurin, and the `Set.uIoc_of_le` rewrite in MediumPNT.
* Everything else is lab-written, including `PNTPort/ZetaZeros/` and `PNTPort/ExplicitPsi1/`.

## Changes made for this repository

* The repository pins Lean and Mathlib v4.35.0-rc3, not the lab's v4.35.0, as every other entry
  here does, because Palomar's Challenge renderer failed on v4.35.0 and later,
  PalomarRegistry/PalomarSubmission issue 161. No lab-written proof needed a change for rc3.
* Every file is in the Lean module system: a `module` header, `public import`, and
  `@[expose] public section`, or `public meta section` for tactic files; the leading comment
  became a module doc comment; `#print axioms` and `#check` lines were removed.
* 45 files are copies of files already in other entries of this repository: from
  `entries/goldstonsuriajaya2021` the 16 ported files, ten `PNTPort/ExplicitPsi1/` files, Edges,
  Final, LeftZeta, Log2Pi, Merom, Nontrivial, Residues, TrivialLimit, TrivialRect and ZeroSum,
  the ten `PNTPort/ZetaZeros/` files and `Common/Goldbach.lean`; `ChebyshevExplicit/Psi30.lean`, `Psi30Upper.lean`,
  `ChenExplicit/GammaBounds.lean`, `Helfgott2013/GammaFine.lean` and `DERZ1997/Statements.lean`
  from `entries/bordignonstarichkova2024grh`; `SchnirelmannConstant/Defs.lean`,
  `Tao2012/SmallRange.lean` and `Tao2012/Statements.lean` from `entries/heathbrown1985`. All are
  byte for byte the same except the two Tao2012 files, whose comments were reworded as below.
  The removals made there are kept: in `DERZ1997/Statements.lean`, `zinoviev`, `derz_lemma`,
  `dtr` and `derz_main`; in `Tao2012/Statements.lean`, the cited statements of Tao's paper.
* Declarations the lab states without proof, as cited results of other papers, were removed
  from this copy. None is used by the compared theorem:
  - `ChirreHagen2025/Statements.lean`: `theorem1`, `theorem2`, `theorem3`, `section5_upper`,
    `noGoldbach_lower`, `lemma2_4`, `lemma2_1`, the cited statements of Chirre and Hagen. The
    definitions there are kept.
  - `RamareSaouter2003/Statements.lean`: `theorem1`, `theorem3`, `theorem2_row59`,
    `corollary1`.
  - `KadiriLumley2014/Statements.lean`: `row_4e18`, `row_45`, `row_60`, `row_150`,
    `corollary1_2`.
* Comments that mentioned the placeholder keyword were reworded, in
  `ChirreHagen2025/Statements.lean`, `RamareSaouter2003/Statements.lean`,
  `KadiriLumley2014/Statements.lean`, `Dudek2014/Statements.lean`, `GoldbachGaps/DudekRH.lean`,
  `Tao2012/SmallRange.lean` and `Tao2012/Statements.lean`. No statement or proof was changed.

## Kernel computations

The closure has only small `decide +kernel` checks: Goldbach for even numbers to 1000 and 1480
in `Common/GoldbachSmall.lean` and `KanieckiGap/Lemma6Gap.lean`, a prime gap scan to 67000 in
`Dudek2014/SmallRange.lean`, and two harmonic number bounds in `Helfgott2013/GammaFine.lean`.
The large files in `ChirreHagen2025/`, `U6743DiagS*`, `U6759DiagSpS*`, `U6759Mid1`, `U6759Mid2`
and `U6806Diag*`, are many short lemmas, interval majorants generated by the lab's scripts and
each closed by `norm_num` and `linarith`; they cost elaboration time and memory, not one large
kernel term.
